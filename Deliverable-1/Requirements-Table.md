# Deliverable 1: Requirements Table

## Problem Statement #64
### Disaster Relief Supply & Volunteer Coordinator

| ID | Type | Description | Priority | Acceptance Criteria | Rationale |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| FR-001 | Functional | The system shall allow the Disaster Manager to register and manage shelter supply requests for essential items such as water, blankets, and medical kits. | High | **Pass:** A shelter request can be entered with required item, quantity, shelter location, and urgency. **Fail:** The system cannot save or update a shelter supply request. | To maintain an organized record of the supplies required at disaster-affected shelters. |
| FR-002 | Functional | The system shall check incoming shelter supply requests against available supplies in the central warehouse. | High | **Pass:** The system displays whether the requested quantity is available in the warehouse. **Fail:** The system provides incorrect or unavailable inventory information. | To ensure that available resources are identified before assigning relief deliveries. |
| FR-003 | Functional | The system shall prioritize shelter supply requests based on the urgency and criticality of the required supplies. | High | **Pass:** Critical requirements such as water and medical kits receive higher dispatch priority than low-priority requests. **Fail:** A low-priority request is dispatched ahead of a critical requirement. | To ensure that the most urgent disaster-relief needs are addressed first. |
| FR-004 | Functional | The system shall assign relief volunteers to tasks based on their registered skills and availability. | Medium | **Pass:** A volunteer with the required skill and availability is assigned to a suitable task. **Fail:** A volunteer is assigned to a task without the required skill or availability. | To ensure that relief activities are performed by appropriately skilled volunteers. |
| FR-005 | Functional | The system shall allow the Disaster Manager to track the status of relief vehicle deliveries from dispatch to delivery. | High | **Pass:** The system displays the current delivery status and updates it when the vehicle progresses. **Fail:** The delivery status cannot be updated or viewed. | To provide visibility into relief deliveries and help the Disaster Manager monitor distribution. |
| NFR-001 | Non-Functional - Performance & Security | The disaster management dashboard shall operate with low network bandwidth consumption and maintain secure access to disaster-relief information. | High | **Pass:** The dashboard remains usable under simulated low-bandwidth conditions and only authorized users can access protected information. **Fail:** The dashboard becomes unusable under low bandwidth or allows unauthorized access. | Disaster-response environments may have limited connectivity, while relief information must remain protected. |
| NFR-002 | Non-Functional - Availability | The system shall support offline caching of essential GIS and disaster-mapping data so that critical information remains accessible during temporary network outages. | High | **Pass:** Previously cached essential map data can be accessed when the network connection is unavailable. **Fail:** Essential cached data cannot be accessed during a network outage. | Disaster areas may have unreliable connectivity, so critical mapping information should remain available when offline. |
